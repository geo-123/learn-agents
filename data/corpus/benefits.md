# Benefits Overview

Acme offers a comprehensive benefits package to all full time employees.

**Healthcare**: company paid medical, dental, and vision coverage from day
one. Dependents covered at 75%. Mental health benefit includes 12 free
therapy sessions per year via Lyra.

**Retirement**: 401(k) in the US, with a 5% employer match. Equivalent
pension contributions in EU offices, country specific.

**Parental leave**: 16 weeks fully paid for the primary caregiver, 8 weeks
for the secondary caregiver. Applies to birth, adoption, and fostering.

**Learning stipend**: $2,000 per year for courses, conferences, books, or
coaching. No preapproval needed for purchases under $500.

**Wellness**: $50/month for gym, yoga, or fitness apps. Reimbursed monthly
via Workfront.
