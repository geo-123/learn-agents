# Vacation Policy

All full time employees at Acme accrue **18 days** of paid vacation per
calendar year, plus 9 company holidays. Accrual happens monthly at 1.5
days per month.

Vacation requests must be submitted in PeopleHQ at least 10 business days
in advance for blocks of 5+ days, and at least 2 business days for shorter
breaks. Manager approval is required.

Unused vacation **does not roll over** to the next year. Up to 5 unused
days may be paid out at end of year at the employee's request.

Sick leave is tracked separately and is uncapped. Sick days do not require
manager approval but should be reported the same morning where possible.
