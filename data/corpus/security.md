# Security Policy

All Acme employees must use the **company password manager** (1Password)
for any work-related credential. Reuse of personal passwords for work
accounts is prohibited.

**MFA is mandatory** for every internal tool. SSO via Okta covers most
applications; tools outside SSO require TOTP or a hardware key.

Customer data must never be copied to a personal device, email, or
cloud account. Use the sanctioned data-room for sharing customer data
internally. Sharing externally requires a signed DPA and Sec-Ops review.

Reporting: if you suspect a phishing attempt, forward to phish@acme.com.
For suspected breaches, page the on-call security engineer via PagerDuty
and notify your manager immediately. **Do not** investigate on your own.

Laptops are full-disk encrypted and remotely wipeable. Lost devices must
be reported to IT within 1 hour.
