# Expense Policy

Acme reimburses reasonable business expenses incurred by employees.
Submit through Workfront within **30 days** of the expense.

**Travel**: economy class for flights under 6 hours; premium economy
allowed for longer. Hotels capped at $300/night ($400 in NYC, London,
Tokyo). Meals capped at $80/day. No upgrades unless paid personally.

**Client entertainment**: requires manager preapproval for any spend
over $200. Itemized receipts mandatory.

**Equipment**: see the Remote Work doc for the home office stipend.
Software licenses and SaaS subscriptions for work require Sec-Ops
review before purchase.

**Receipts**: required for all expenses over $25. For meals over $25,
include the names of all attendees in the memo field.

Expenses without receipts or over the caps require VP approval and a
written justification.
