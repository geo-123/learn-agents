# Remote Work and Hybrid Schedule

Acme operates on a **hybrid by default** model. Employees assigned to the
Lisbon, London, or NYC offices are expected to be on site **two days per
week**, typically Tuesday and Thursday. The remaining days are remote.

Fully remote roles exist for engineering, design, and customer support,
but require VP level approval and a documented business case. Fully remote
employees still receive a quarterly travel stipend to visit a hub office.

Working from outside your home country requires HR notification and is
limited to **30 days per year** for tax and visa reasons. Notify HR at
least 5 business days before you travel.

Home office equipment stipend: $1,500 every 3 years, redeemable through
Workfront.
